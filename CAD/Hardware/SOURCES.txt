Cubi 2 - where the models in CAD/hardware/ come from
===================================================

These models were made by other authors. They are NOT covered by the Cubi 2
licences (LICENSE, LICENSE-CAD.md); each keeps its own licence, given below.
Keep this file with them when you share them.


1. Open electronics 3D library (STEP)
   Source : github.com/KiCad/kicad-packages3D
   Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 with an exception - free to use in your designs; your
            design does NOT have to take the same licence.

   CP_Radial_D10.0mm_P5.00mm.step           electrolytic capacitor 220 uF / 50 V
   D_DO-201AD_P15.24mm_Horizontal.step      TVS diode 1.5KE30CA
   D_DO-41_SOD81_P10.16mm_Horizontal.step   small axial part (fuse, 10.16 pitch)
   PinHeader_1x06_P2.54mm_Vertical.step     6-pin header

2. mcu.step - the MCU board
   Source : github.com/blackketter/teensy.pretty  (author: Zack Kummer)
   Licence: MIT
   Measured: 66.56 x 17.78 x 5.27 mm (the USB socket sticks out 4.08 mm).

3. Bought parts, from their makers' published models
   motor_src.step          the motor, as published by its maker
   motor_driver_src.step   the motor driver, as published by its maker
   imu.step                the IMU board, as published by its maker
   These are used for fit and assembly only. Check each maker's terms before
   you share them; if in doubt, leave them out and let users download them.

4. Made for Cubi 2 (Cubi 2 licence, CC BY 4.0)
   motor.step              motor_src.step with the real colours
   motor_driver.step       motor_driver_src.step + its terminal block
   bconn_male.step,
   bconn_female.step       the battery connector pair, from its drawing
   spacer_OD6_ID3.2_H1.step  ready-made IMU spacer (drawn for Cubi 2)
   board_x.step, board_y.step, board_z.step, perfboard.step
                           the three electronics boards
