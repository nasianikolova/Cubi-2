# Respect and safety

Cubi 2 was made so that high-school students can build a real space-style robot
and learn physics, mathematics, programming and engineering on it. The licences
(`LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CAD.md`) make it free for everyone. This page is not an
extra licence condition; it is how the project asks to be treated.

## In the spirit of the project

- **Keep the name.** Call it Cubi 2, or say clearly that your work is based on
  Cubi 2, and credit the authors.
- **Be honest about changes.** If you change it, say what you changed, so nobody
  confuses your version with the original or blames the original for your changes.
- **Keep it open for learning.** If you build on it, please share your
  improvements back so that other classrooms benefit.
- **Respect the students.** Work made by students - their builds, code, photos,
  results - belongs to them. Credit them by name only with their permission (and,
  for minors, with their guardians' permission).
- **Respect people.** Use Cubi 2 to teach, include and encourage - never to
  mock, exclude, harm or mislead anyone.

## Safety - for students under supervision

Cubi 2 is meant to be built and used by high-school students **under the
supervision of a teacher or mentor**. It contains things that can hurt or burn
if handled carelessly:

- a **6S lithium-polymer battery** (22.2 V, very high current): charge only with a
  proper balance charger, never unattended, never puncture, crush or short it;
  store and charge it in a fire-safe bag
- **fast-spinning reaction wheels**: keep fingers, hair and cables away while the
  motors are powered; secure the cube before testing
- a **soldering iron** and **heat-set inserts** (about 210 °C): use a stand, work
  in a ventilated space, protect eyes
- **strong magnets** and **super glue**: magnets pinch fingers and harm
  electronics and cards; glue bonds skin in seconds
- **3D-printed parts** in carbon-fibre filament: do not sand without a mask

The supervising adult decides what each student may do. Everything is provided
"as is", without warranty (see the licences).
