## Build status

Printing, assembly and the build documents have been tried on real builds. The **operation of the cube has not been tested yet**. Dimensions, clearances, screw and insert sizes, print settings and weights come from tests and real builds, but they **may still contain errors**. Check every number against your own parts, printer and materials before you rely on it.

Parts of the build documents are produced with the help of **artificial intelligence**. They are reviewed, but they may contain mistakes. Treat them as a record, not as an authority.

## Safety and responsibility

The safety notes in `RESPECT.md` are a summary, not a complete risk assessment. Cubi 2 involves a **6S lithium-polymer battery**, **fast spinning reaction wheels**, **soldering at high temperature**, **strong magnets**, **adhesives** and **carbon-fibre filament**. These can cause fire, burns, cuts, crushing, eye damage and inhalation of harmful particles.

- The person who builds, charges, powers or operates a Cubi 2 — and, for students, the **supervising adult, school or organisation** — carries full responsibility for safe working, risk assessment, supervision, protective equipment, insurance and compliance with local rules and school policy.
- Anyone using this material must have, or be supervised by someone who has, the skills to judge whether a step is safe in their own setting.
- The supervising adult decides what each student may do.
